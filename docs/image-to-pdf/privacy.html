Privacy Policy

Image to PDF: Photo Converter · Effective October 16, 2026

This policy explains how Image to PDF handles information when you use the app.

Photos and PDFs

Photos you select are processed locally on your iPhone to create a PDF. The app does not upload your photos or PDFs to us or to a server. The app does not keep a cloud copy. You choose whether to share the resulting PDF or save it somewhere using the iOS share sheet.

Information collection

The app does not require an account and does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, contacts, location, or other personal information. It includes no advertising or tracking SDKs.

Photo access

Image selection uses Apple’s system photo picker. You choose which images to provide to the app; the app does not need access to your full photo library.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a revised effective date. For privacy questions, email 857377735@qq.com. See also the support page.
