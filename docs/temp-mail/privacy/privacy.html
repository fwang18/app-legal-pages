Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026.

Temp Mail does not collect data for the developer, run analytics, show ads, or require an account with us.

To provide a working inbox, the app connects directly to mail.tm, a third-party temporary email service. Your temporary email address and messages are received and stored on that service's servers for a limited time before deletion according to its policies. The app stores the current address and access credentials on your device so you can reopen your inbox. We do not operate an email server or receive copies of your messages.

Anyone who knows your temporary address can send mail to it. Avoid using temporary mail for sensitive information or accounts you need to recover later. Replacing an address deletes its account through the service and cannot be undone.
