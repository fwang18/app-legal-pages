Privacy, plainly.

AI Prompt Library · Last updated October 17, 2026

AI Prompt Library is designed to work on your device. It does not ask for an account and does not send your prompt activity anywhere.

Your saved prompts, favorites, and edits are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to us. Removing the app may remove this local information.
