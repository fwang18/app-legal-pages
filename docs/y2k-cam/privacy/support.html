Need a hand?

We’re happy to help with the Y2K photo editor.

Using the editor

Choose a photo with Choose Photo or take one with Camera. Tap a look to preview it. Use the date row to choose a date, then turn the stamp on or off. Save the finished image to Photos or share it from the share button.

Permissions

Y2K asks for camera access only when you take a photo and photo-library add access only when you save. If you denied access earlier, you can turn it on in iOS Settings.
