Common questions

Does the app book for me? No. It opens the official DMV page, fills in your details, and shows the dates the page lists. You pick a time and confirm on the DMV page.

Will it alert me about new openings automatically? No. Dates are only checked while you have the official page open. You can turn on a daily reminder to look again.

The form wasn't filled in. Tap “Fill my details” once the contact form is on screen. If the DMV changed its page, use ⋯ › Copy my details and paste them yourself.

Is this the DMV's app? No. It is an unofficial app, not affiliated with the California DMV or the State of California. For questions about your appointment, contact the DMV.
