Privacy Policy

DMV Appointment Finder: CA · Last updated October 19, 2026

Summary

We do not collect any data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs.

Details you save in the app

Your name, email, phone number and, if you choose, your driver's license or permit number and date of birth are stored only in your iPhone's Keychain. They are used only to fill in the official California DMV booking form when you open it inside the app. They are never sent to us.

Other data on your device

Your saved offices, your target date, the appointment dates the official page showed you, and walk-in wait times are stored only on your device.

Connections the app makes

  • The official booking page on dmv.ca.gov, which you open and use yourself. What you enter there is governed by the DMV's own privacy policy.
  • Public DMV office information and office pages on dmv.ca.gov, to show office addresses, hours and walk-in wait times.

The app does not send your personal details anywhere except into the official form you are looking at.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone.

Deleting your data

Tap “Delete my details” in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Not affiliated

This is an unofficial app. It is not affiliated with the California DMV or the State of California.
