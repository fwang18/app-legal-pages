Body Editor Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Body Editor processes the images you select on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share your images or personal data. The app has no account, analytics, advertising, third-party SDK, or network service.

When you choose to save an edited image, iOS may ask for permission to add it to your photo library. You can change this permission in iOS Settings. Sharing uses the iOS share sheet and happens only when you request it.
