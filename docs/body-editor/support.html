Body Editor Support

Choose an image with Open. Select Slim, Enhance, or Taller, drag the circle or horizontal guide, adjust the sliders, then tap Apply. Use Undo or Redo to revisit edits. Touch and hold Compare to see the original.

To save, tap the download icon and allow Add Photos access. To send an edited image to another app, tap Share. If saving fails, check Body Editor's photo access in iOS Settings.
