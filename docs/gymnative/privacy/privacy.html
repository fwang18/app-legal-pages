Setsheet Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Setsheet is a workout log for iPhone. In short: your data stays on your iPhone, and we don't collect, see or share any of it.

What the app stores

Your plans, routines, workouts, personal records, body weight entries and settings are saved in a file inside the app on your device. They are included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud or computer) if you have those turned on, under your own Apple account.

What we collect

Nothing. Setsheet has no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send your data to us or to anyone else, and it does not make network requests.

Backups you export

When you choose “Export backup”, the app creates a JSON file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where that file goes (Files, AirDrop, email, another app) is your choice, and it is then handled by that destination.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local notifications on your iPhone for the rest timer and workout reminders. They are created on the device; no notification server is involved.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Setsheet 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 23 日

Setsheet 是一个 iPhone 上的训练记录 App。简单说：你的数据只存在你的 iPhone 上，我们不收集、看不到，也不会分享。

App 保存什么

计划、训练日、训练记录、个人纪录、体重和设置，都保存在本机 App 内的一个文件里。如果你开了 iPhone 备份（iCloud 或电脑），它们会随你自己的 Apple 账户一起备份。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。没有账户、没有登录、没有统计分析、没有广告、没有追踪。App 不会把你的数据发给我们或任何人，也不发起网络请求。

你导出的备份

选「导出备份」时，App 生成一个 JSON 文件交给系统分享面板。存到哪里（文件、隔空投送、邮件、其他 App）由你决定，之后由对方处理。

通知

如果你允许通知，App 会在本机安排休息倒计时和训练提醒的本地通知，不经过任何服务器。

删除数据

删除 App 即删除它在你 iPhone 上的全部数据。
