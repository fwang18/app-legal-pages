Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Everything is stored on your iPhone.
How do I move my data to a new phone?
Settings → Data & backup → Export backup (JSON), save the file, then Import backup on the new phone. A normal iPhone backup or transfer also carries it over.
How do I switch between kg and lb?
Settings → Units & language → Weight unit. The app asks whether to convert the numbers you already logged or only change the unit label.
How do I change the language?
Settings → Units & language → Language. 19 languages are available.
The rest timer doesn't alert me in the background.
Allow notifications for Setsheet in iOS Settings → Notifications.
