Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

TExES PPR Practice is an independent, offline study app. It does not require an account, connect to a server, include advertising, or use third-party analytics.

The app stores your practice progress, missed questions, and rating-request milestones locally on your device using iOS storage. We do not collect, transmit, or sell this information. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to any device backup settings you control.

The optional “Rate this app” action uses Apple's App Store. Apple's own policies govern information you provide there. The app's support and privacy links open external web pages only when you choose them.
