Privacy Policy

Global Entry Interview Alert · Last updated October 19, 2026

Summary

We do not collect any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs.

What stays on your phone

The enrollment centers you star, your cutoff date and the last results the app saw are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network requests

To find open interview slots, the app sends anonymous requests to the public interview schedule (the list of enrollment centers and the soonest open slot for a center number). These requests contain no personal information. The schedule operator may see your IP address as with any website visit. We do not run a server and receive nothing.

Notifications

Alerts are local notifications created on your device. No push server is involved.

Your logins

The app never asks for, stores or uses your login for any website. You sign in and book on the official website yourself.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Not official

This is an independent app, not affiliated with U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) or any government agency.
