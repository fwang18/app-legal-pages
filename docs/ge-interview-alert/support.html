Global Entry Interview Alert tells you when an enrollment center you picked has an interview opening earlier than your date. You then book it yourself on the official website.

Getting started

  1. Tap +, search a city or state, and star the centers you could get to (Global Entry, NEXUS or SENTRI).
  2. Set Alert me if earlier than to the date you'd like to beat.
  3. Allow notifications. When an earlier opening appears, tap the alert to open the official scheduling site, sign in and book it.

FAQ

How often does it check? Every time you open the app (each center at most once every 5 minutes). In the background, iOS decides how often the app may run, which can be only a few times a day. Open the app for a fresh check.

The opening was gone when I signed in. Openings are often taken within minutes. Keep alerts on and try again on the next one.

Can the app book for me? No. It never signs in and never books, holds or sells appointments.

It says the schedule isn't responding. The public schedule is sometimes down for maintenance. Try again later.

I got my interview. Open the center and tap I booked it to pause alerts. Congratulations!
